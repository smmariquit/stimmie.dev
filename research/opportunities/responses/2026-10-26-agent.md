# Weekly research — 2026-10-26 (scheduled agent run)

## Method

Ran three parallel research passes: (1) hackathons and game jams, (2)
internships, programs, and scholarships, (3) events, certificates, and calls
for speakers/mentors/volunteers. Each pass ran WebSearch queries across
Devpost, itch.io, MLH, university/GDG hackathons, PH government programs
(DOST, DICT, DTI), accelerators, fellowships, UPOU MODeL, Luma/Meetup PH tech
listings, and Friends of Figma / DEVCON / AWSUG event pages, then verified
every candidate by fetching the live page directly. Every surviving candidate
was then independently re-fetched a second time (via WebFetch and curl with
a browser user agent) before publishing, cross-checked against
`src/data/opportunity-board-items.js` (319 existing URLs, 322 existing
titles extracted) to avoid re-adding duplicates, and required at least one
date in the future relative to 2026-10-26.
