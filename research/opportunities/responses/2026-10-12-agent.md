# Weekly research — 2026-10-12 (scheduled agent run)

## Method

Ran targeted WebSearch queries across hackathons, game jams, internships,
scholarships, exchange programs, certificates, and call-for-speaker/mentor/
volunteer listings relevant to Philippine students and early-career builders.
Verified every candidate by fetching the live page (HTTP 200, browser UA
required for Devpost subdomains which block default fetchers), checking
eligibility/country-exclusion clauses in official rules pages, and confirming
at least one date in the future relative to 2026-10-12. Cross-checked every
candidate against `src/data/opportunity-board-items.js` (315 existing URLs
extracted) to avoid re-adding duplicates.
